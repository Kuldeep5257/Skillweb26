Course Overview

What this course is designed to teach

Practical computerized accounting training covering Tally Prime, ledgers, vouchers, inventory, GST workflows, banking, and business reports. The curriculum below is organized as an easy-to-update outline. Exact module order, batch duration and included software can be confirmed with the academy before admission.

Syllabus Highlights

What you will study

✓
Accounting Foundations

Basic accounting terms, debit/credit concepts and business transaction flow.

✓
Company Setup

Create companies, financial periods, security settings and configuration basics.

✓
Groups & Ledgers

Create and organize ledgers, groups and account structures.

✓
Accounting Vouchers

Payment, receipt, contra, journal, purchase and sales entries.

✓
Inventory

Stock groups, items, units, locations and inventory transaction basics.

✓
GST Workflows

GST-oriented configuration, invoices and common transaction-entry practice.

✓
Banking & Payroll Basics

Bank reconciliation awareness, payroll concepts and routine business records.

✓
Reports & Practice

Trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, outstanding reports and practical business cases.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the program, you should be able to

  • ✓Understand the flow of computerized accounting in Tally Prime.
  • ✓Create companies, ledgers, vouchers and inventory records.
  • ✓Enter common purchase, sales, receipt and payment transactions.
  • ✓Work with GST-oriented business entries at a practical level.
  • ✓Read core accounting and business reports generated by Tally Prime.
