About Skill Sphere Academy

Skill-based computer training built to bridge learning and industry needs.

Skill Sphere Academy is located in Chauhan Patti, North East Delhi. Its programs are designed around practical learning, real-world projects, regularly updated curriculum, modern tools, personal attention, and career-development support.

Our Mission

To deliver practical, industry-relevant computer education with hands-on learning, live projects, and career-focused support.

Our Vision

To help bridge the gap between academic learning and current industry requirements through modern digital education.
