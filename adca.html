Course Overview

What this course is designed to teach

An advanced computer applications program designed to extend office, spreadsheet, accounting, design, web, and system skills. The curriculum below is organized as an easy-to-update outline. Exact module order, batch duration and included software can be confirmed with the academy before admission.

Syllabus Highlights

What you will study

✓
Advanced Office Tools

Word, PowerPoint and professional productivity workflows beyond the basics.

✓
Advanced Excel

Functions, lookup logic, charts, data cleaning, PivotTables and reporting techniques.

✓
Tally Prime Concepts

Company setup, ledgers, vouchers, inventory and business-accounting workflows.

✓
GST & Business Records

Practical GST-oriented entries and business documentation concepts.

✓
Desktop Publishing Basics

Page layout, basic design principles and print-ready document creation.

✓
Web Fundamentals

HTML/CSS concepts and an introduction to how websites are structured.

✓
Hardware & Networking Basics

Computer components, troubleshooting awareness and simple networking concepts.

✓
Capstone Practice

A final practical project combining multiple applications and workplace tasks.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the program, you should be able to

  • ✓Handle a wider range of office and computer-application tasks.
  • ✓Use Excel at a stronger intermediate/advanced level.
  • ✓Understand common computerized accounting and business workflows.
  • ✓Gain introductory exposure to design, web and system concepts.
  • ✓Develop a broader skill set for office, support and entry-level computer roles.
